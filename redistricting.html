Efficiency gap computed from certified two-party votes per Stephanopoulos & McGhee (2015): (wasted D votes − wasted R votes) ÷ total two-party votes; positive values favor Republicans. Uncontested races are imputed as a 75/25 split of the state's median contested-district turnout. Only states with 7+ districts are shown — the measure is unstable for small delegations.

District boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files for the 114th–119th Congresses. Election results: MIT Election Data and Science Lab, U.S. House constituency returns (CC0), 2014–2018; FEC "Federal Elections" official publications, 2020–2022; 2024 winners from the unitedstates/congress-legislators project (margins pending certified-returns integration). Racial demographics: U.S. Census Bureau ACS 5-Year Estimates (2021 and 2022 vintages, matched to each map era's boundaries). Ranked-choice races (AK, ME) use final-round tallies; Louisiana races decided in runoffs use runoff tallies.
